# Code of Conduct

Legacy Player follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The official text is the one that applies; this page is a short summary and says how to reach us.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Legacy Player (issues, pull requests, discussions, and the friends services and servers people
run with it) to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to emulators, networking or code.
- Respect different opinions and experiences; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and take helpful criticism gracefully; own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the players and the project, not only for yourself.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information (for example their address, their IP address, a server code or a friend
  code they did not make public) without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

Asking for, or sharing, copyrighted game files, ROMs, ISOs or BIOS files is also not allowed here (see
[docs/LEGAL_POSITION.md](docs/LEGAL_POSITION.md)).

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@Alpallyoop](https://github.com/Alpallyoop), privately: use
GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" form under the repository's Security tab (it reaches the maintainer only), or
GitHub's own "Report content" / "Report abuse" option on the comment or user. Every report is reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Abuse inside a friends service or game server is handled by whoever runs that service, with the in-app report,
block and moderation tools ([docs/FRIENDS_SECURITY.md](docs/FRIENDS_SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, in line with the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines),
respond with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated
the behaviour is.
